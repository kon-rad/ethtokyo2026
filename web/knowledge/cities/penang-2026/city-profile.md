# Penang 2026 — City Profile

**Location:** George Town, Penang, Malaysia
**Dates:** June 1 – June 14, 2026
**Mission:** Explore the intersection of tropical living, decentralized coordination, and Southeast Asian culture.
**Core team:** Maya Chen (founder), Alex Rivera, Siti Hassan

## About

Penang 2026 is a two-week pop-up city in the UNESCO heritage zone of George Town. We gather in a restored shophouse with a courtyard, walking distance from street food, galleries, and the sea. Days are for workshops, co-working, and exploration. Evenings are for shared meals and spontaneous sessions.

## Venue

The main gathering space is Rumah Kongsi, a restored clan house on Muntri Street. It has:
- Open courtyard (seats ~40)
- Co-working loft with fiber internet
- Rooftop terrace overlooking the city
- Outdoor kitchen

## Core values

- Consent before photos or content creation
- Leave no trace in shared spaces
- Pay what you can for community meals (suggested RM15-25)
- English is the common language, but Malay, Mandarin, and Tamil are welcome
