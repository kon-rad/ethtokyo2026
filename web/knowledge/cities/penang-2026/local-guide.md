# Local Guide — Penang 2026

## Transport

- **From airport (PEN):** Grab ~45 min / RM35-45. Taxi counter ~RM55.
- **Within George Town:** Walking is best. Grab and RapidPenang buses cover longer trips.
- **Bike rental:** Several shops on Chulia Street, ~RM15/day.
- **Ferry to mainland:** RM1.20 from Pengkalan Raja Tun Uda.

## Food

- **Best nasi kandar:** Line Clear (Jalan Penang) — 24h
- **Vegetarian:** Woodlands (Jalan Penang) — North Indian, cheap
- **Nyonya:** Ivan's (Jalan Nagor) — book ahead
- **Cheap eats:** Cecil Street Market, Air Itam pasar malam (Mon)
- **Coffee:** Norm Micro Roastery, Brick & Mortar

## Culture

- **Heritage zone** is UNESCO-listed. Walk Jalan Masjid Kapitan Keling for the harmony of mosque, temple, and church on one street.
- **Street art** is everywhere — Ernest Zacharevic murals are the famous ones, but the real gems are the hidden ones in side lanes.
- **Night market** at Batu Ferringhi (Fri-Sat) — touristy but fun for one visit.

## Weather

- June: 28-32°C, very humid. Afternoon tropical downpours are common (30-60 min).
- Bring: light cotton clothes, rain jacket, hat, reef-safe sunscreen.
- Air conditioning is not guaranteed in heritage buildings. Fans everywhere.

## SIM & connectivity

- Celcom, Digi, Maxis — all sell tourist eSIMs at the airport (~RM30 for 7 days, 10GB).
